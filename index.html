ABOUT US

About Blackwood Furnitures

We are a premium furniture manufacturer based in Nairobi, dedicated to crafting exceptional pieces for homes, offices, and hotels. Our story began with a passion for quality materials and skilled workmanship that has been honed over 15 years of serving the Kenyan market.

Every piece we create is a testament to our commitment to excellence — from the initial design consultation to the final delivery and installation. We take pride in our ability to transform raw materials into furniture that not only looks beautiful but also stands the test of time.

Whether you're a homeowner looking for a custom sofa, a business needing professional office furniture, or a developer requiring bulk supply for a hospitality project, Blackwood Furnitures is your trusted partner.
