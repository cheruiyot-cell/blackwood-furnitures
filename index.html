QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK BEFORE THEY ORDER

Answers Before You Have To Ask

Everything you'd want to know before committing. No surprises.

How much does custom furniture cost?
A 3-seater sofa starts at KES 45,000. A queen bed frame with headboard starts at KES 38,000. Executive desks start at KES 28,000. A 3-door wardrobe starts at KES 55,000. These are real starting prices — your final quote depends on size, timber and fabric, and we send it in writing within 24 hours.
How long does a custom order take?
3–4 weeks from deposit for standard pieces. Full office or hotel fit-outs take 6–10 weeks. We give you a written delivery date before you pay anything.
What's the payment structure?
50% deposit to start, 50% on delivery. You get a signed contract and receipt for every payment. We never ask for full payment upfront.
What if it doesn't fit my space?
We measure your space ourselves before designing — free, anywhere in Nairobi. You approve a 3D drawing before we cut any timber. If it doesn't fit on delivery day, that's on us, not you.
Do you deliver to my area?
Yes — free delivery and installation within Nairobi metro (20km of Huruma). Outside that radius, we quote delivery upfront so there's no surprise on the invoice.
Can I see the furniture before I pay?
Yes. Visit our workshop in Huruma to see timber, fabric samples and finished pieces. We'll send the exact location and a landmark on WhatsApp when you book a visit.
What if I don't like the finished piece?
You approve the design before we build, so surprises are rare. If something isn't to the agreed spec, we correct it. Every piece carries a 12-month workmanship warranty.
